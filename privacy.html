VoltWatch Privacy Policy

Effective July 25, 2026

VoltWatch does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Data stored on Apple Watch

VoltWatch stores saved BMS device names and identifiers, battery readings and history, alert thresholds, and app preferences locally on the user’s Apple Watch. This information is used only to provide battery-monitoring features.

Bluetooth

VoltWatch uses Bluetooth Low Energy to discover and communicate with nearby compatible battery-management systems. Bluetooth identifiers and battery telemetry are processed on the user’s Apple Watch and are not sent to the developer or a third party.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

VoltWatch has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, cross-app tracking technology, account system, or developer-operated server.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store purchases under Apple’s terms and privacy policy. VoltWatch does not receive payment-card information.

Deleting data

Deleting VoltWatch from Apple Watch removes its locally stored app data.

Children

VoltWatch does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date and publishing a revised policy at this address.
